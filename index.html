Friends · Family · Market groups

Set the contribution terms

One circle uses exactly one currency or asset for its lifetime — it can't be changed later.

Step 4 of 6 · Circle Start

When does this circle start?

Creating the circle and starting its first cycle are two different moments.

Start on a specific date
Members can begin contributing from that date. The circle is Scheduled until then (or Active immediately, if the date is today).
I'll start it manually
The circle is created in Setup / Recruiting. Add members first, then start the first cycle yourself whenever you're ready.

Step 5 of 6 · Payment Details

Where will members send their contributions?

These payment details are the destination members will be instructed to use for this Circle. This is your own payment destination — KUNTRIBUTE does not hold or move funds.
